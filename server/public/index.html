FAQ

Questions

Do I need Forge or Fabric?

No. Astra is a complete client. The app sets up everything it needs by itself, so you don't install a mod loader or manage mod files.

Can I use it on Hypixel and other servers?

Astra only includes visual and quality-of-life features. There's no reach, autoclicker or x-ray. Every server sets its own rules, so check a server's allowed-modifications list if you're unsure.

Will it mess with my normal Minecraft?

No. Astra keeps its own copy of the game, worlds and settings in its own folder. Your .minecraft folder and the official launcher stay exactly as they are.

Is my account safe?

You sign in through Microsoft's own page. Astra keeps only a sign-in token, encrypted with Windows' data protection, never your password.

Can other players see my cosmetics?

Yes. Everyone using Astra sees your equipped cape and wings, and you see theirs. Players on other clients see the normal game.

Does it cost anything?

No. Astra, its app and its cosmetics are free.

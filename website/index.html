How it works

From download to in game in three steps

Astra handles the setup a modded install normally needs. You never install a mod loader, find the right Java version or drop files into a mods folder.

  1. Install Astra

    Run the installer. The Astra app keeps everything in its own folder, so your normal .minecraft and the official launcher stay untouched.

  2. Sign in with Microsoft

    Use the account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition. It's the same Microsoft sign-in the official launcher uses, and Astra never sees your password.

  3. Pick a version and launch

    Choose 1.8.9 or 1.7.10 and press Launch. The first time, Astra downloads Java and the game for you. After that it starts straight away.

Built in

Everything lives in one menu

Press Right Shift in game. Every feature is a card with a live preview: click to turn it on, open its options to make it yours.

  • HUD editor. Drag anything anywhere. It snaps to the edges and the center.
  • Make it yours. Colors, size, backgrounds and keybinds for every feature.
  • Remembers you. Your layout and settings are saved for each version.

Cosmetics

FAQ

Questions

Do I need Forge or Fabric?

No. Astra is a complete client. The app sets up everything it needs by itself, so you don't install a mod loader or manage mod files.

Can I use it on Hypixel and other servers?

Astra only includes visual and quality-of-life features. There's no reach, autoclicker or x-ray. Every server sets its own rules, so check a server's allowed-modifications list if you're unsure.

Will it mess with my normal Minecraft?

No. Astra keeps its own copy of the game, worlds and settings in its own folder. Your .minecraft folder and the official launcher stay exactly as they are.

Is my account safe?

You sign in through Microsoft's own page. Astra keeps only a sign-in token, encrypted with Windows' data protection, never your password.

Can other players see my cosmetics?

For now they show on your own player, in third person and in your inventory. Showing them to other Astra players is planned.

Does it cost anything?

No. Astra, its app and its cosmetics are free.
